# References for the CS6515 (OMSCS Graduate Algorithms) final-exam study guide

Prefetched on 2026-11-26. Each line: URL | what it is | what it says (summarised).

- https://omscs.gatech.edu/sites/default/files/documents/Syllabi/CS%206515%202026-3.pdf | Fall 2026 syllabus (official) | Topics: dynamic programming; divide and conquer; randomized algorithms incl. RSA; graph algorithms; max-flow; linear programming; NP-completeness. Grade: Exams 90% (Exam 1 10%, Exam 2 10%, Exam 3 20%, Exam 4 20%) + Cumulative MCQ Final Exam 30% with up to 10% extra credit; quizzes 10%; homework 0%. Cutoffs A 85%+, B 70%, C 50%, no rounding.
- https://www.omscs.gatech.edu/cs-6515-intro-graduate-algorithms | OMSCS course page | Units: DP, divide & conquer, graph algorithms, max-flow, linear programming, NP complexity theory. Prerequisite: DFS, BFS, Dijkstra, basic DP and D&C including recurrences.
- https://www.cc.gatech.edu/~vigoda/GA | Vigoda's lecture outline (the course lectures) | Lecture units DP (Fib, LIS, LCS, knapsack, chain multiply, shortest paths), DC (fast multiplication, median, FFT), RA (modular arithmetic, RSA, primality), GR (SCC, 2-SAT, MST), MF (max flow, min cut), LP (duality, simplex), NP (reductions, halting problem).
- https://blog-5nd.pages.dev/posts/cs-650-graduate-algorithms/ | A student's course review | Earlier term split: Exam 1 DP, D&C, FFT; Exam 2 SCC, MST, max flow, modular math / RSA; Exam 3 NP-completeness, LP, halting problem. Learn the required answer format; homework and quizzes are the best exam practice.
- https://awaisrauf.github.io/omscs_reviews/CS-6515 | Aggregated student reviews | Practice problems and Joves's notes are the most recommended prep; for multiple-choice, review the lectures and the DPV book chapters.
- https://brainscape.com/flashcards/cs6515_exam3-11648987/packs/20156545 | Student flashcards (exam 3) | LP is in P; ILP is NP-complete; NP-complete problems are the hardest in NP, assuming P != NP.
- https://courses.cs.washington.edu/courses/cse421/24sp/final-review.pdf | A comparable algorithms final review (UW CSE421) | Topic checklist: asymptotics, greedy, D&C and master theorem, DP, network flow, LP (standard form, primal-dual), NP (reductions, VC, IS, clique, set cover).

Textbook used by the course: Dasgupta, Papadimitriou, Vazirani, "Algorithms" (DPV).
